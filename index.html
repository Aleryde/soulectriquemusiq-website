Soul, meaning the human current. Électrique, meaning the machine that carries it. Soulectrique is the point where the two meet. A label built to run one through the other since 2014.

EXPLORE RELEASES
01 — THE LABEL

Signal over noise.

Soulectrique is a crossing of languages before it's a crossing of sound — soul, in English, and électrique, in French, meeting in a single current.

The q is the quality and uniqueness factor of the track — the frequency that stays one of a kind, unrepeatable, recognized on contact. It's the mark that completes soulectrique: an international, electric soul, measured not in perfection, but in truth.
